Privacy Policy

YorpHives provides free browser utilities and information about HQventures products. This notice describes the website’s current privacy practices and explains how advertising will be handled if enabled in the future.

Free Tool inputs and files

Many Free Tools run directly inside your browser. For those tools, your input or selected files are processed locally rather than uploaded to YorpHives. Processing methods vary by tool; read the notice on each tool before using it. Do not submit sensitive information to a tool that identifies an external service or upload.

Anonymous website analytics

YorpHives uses first-party usage measurements to understand which pages and tools are useful. The analytics client is designed not to send tool inputs, file names, processed outputs, cookies, or device fingerprints. It can record page views, tool-use events, and a temporary browser-tab session identifier. Where available, you can disable anonymous analytics using the site's privacy control, and browser Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals are respected by that client. Servers and infrastructure providers may still process technical request information needed to deliver and secure the service.

Contact and enquiries

If you contact YorpHives, information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, and message, may be processed to respond to your enquiry. Avoid sending passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information. Hosting and security systems may process limited technical information to prevent abuse.

Advertising

Google AdSense is currently pending site review; YorpHives has not enabled advertising placements. If advertising is introduced, it is intended only for the Free Tools section. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertisements, subject to applicable consent requirements. This policy and any required consent controls will be updated before advertising is enabled. Learn more about Google's data use at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Service providers and retention

Infrastructure providers, including the current hosting platform, process data necessary to serve and protect the website. Information retained for analytics, abuse prevention, or enquiries may have different retention periods. You may contact us to ask questions about available data or request deletion where applicable.

Your choices

You can refrain from submitting personal information, use browser privacy controls, and disable optional analytics where the website offers that setting. If advertising is enabled in the future, additional region-specific consent choices may be provided.

Contact

For privacy questions, email connect@yorphives.com.
