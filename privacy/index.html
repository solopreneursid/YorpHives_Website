Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how YorpHives handles information when you visit yorphives.com, use its Free Tools or public tool API, or contact us through HQconnect. It also explains how we will give notice for future HQventures products.

The short version

Browser-local tools process your inputs on your device. Optional website analytics stay off until you allow them. If you send an enquiry or use a server-side API, the information you submit is sent to the service for that purpose.

Who we are

YorpHives operates this website, its Free Tools and HQconnect. HQventures is the YorpHives area for projects and products. For privacy questions or requests, contact connect@yorphives.com.

Free Tools and your device

Tools marked as running in your browser process the text, files, images, audio or video you select on your device. Those inputs and results are not uploaded to YorpHives as part of that browser-local processing. Some tools first download their software or AI model files from our website to your device.

Your browser may ask for permission before a recording tool can use your microphone or share a screen. You control that permission through your browser and can revoke it there. Files you download or save remain on your device until you remove them. Your browser, operating system or extensions may handle information under their own settings.

Read the notice for the particular tool before using it. The separate public API described below works differently from browser-local tools.

Public tool API

If you or software acting for you sends a request to our public server-side tool API, the request and its input are sent to YorpHives' infrastructure to produce a response. The application is designed not to save the input or output, and records only the supported tool's identifier and a count for operational statistics. Infrastructure providers may still process technical request information for delivery and security. Do not send passwords, sensitive documents or other confidential information to a public API.

Optional analytics and local storage

With your permission, our first-party analytics count page visits and completed tool uses so we can understand and improve the website. They use a random identifier scoped to a browser tab; the application stores a hash of that identifier and daily counts. These are privacy-conscious, pseudonymous measurements, not a guarantee that all information is anonymous. The analytics client is designed not to send your tool inputs, filenames or results, and does not use an advertising pixel or device fingerprint.

Analytics are off until you choose Allow analytics. You can decline without losing access to the tools, or turn analytics off at any time using the analytics control in the page footer or below. We also respect the browser's Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal for this analytics client.

Your analytics choice

Optional analytics are off until you choose to allow them.

We use browser storage to remember your analytics choice and, if analytics are allowed, a tab-scoped session identifier. A saved-tools feature also stores your selected tool shortcuts in your browser. These are separate from your tool inputs. Clearing browser storage can remove your choices and saved shortcuts; choices apply separately to each browser or device.

HQconnect enquiries and email

When you submit an HQconnect form, we receive the details you enter: your name, email, request type and message, and any project name, preferred timeline or budget range you provide. We also record a submission time, reference, consent to contact and an enquiry status. We use these details to review your request, reply and manage a possible project or partnership.

Enquiries are stored in a private database available through the founder's protected headquarters. An operational notification may say that an enquiry is waiting without including its contents. If you email us directly, your email provider and ours process the messages. Please do not send passwords, payment-card information, identity documents or other sensitive information in an initial enquiry.

Service providers, security and transfers

We currently use Cloudflare to host and deliver the website and to provide database, security and operational services. Cloudflare and other communications providers involved in a service may process technical information such as IP address, request details and security signals to deliver, protect or troubleshoot it. Access to enquiries is restricted, but no online service can promise perfect security.

Providers may process information in countries other than the one you are in. Applicable laws may require safeguards for international transfers; contact us if you would like more information about a particular transfer. Links to other websites are governed by those sites' own privacy notices.

How long information is kept

Our analytics application is designed to remove session and usage records older than approximately 35 days during routine report checks; a technical interruption may delay that cleanup. Providers' security records and any backups can have different retention periods.

HQconnect enquiries and correspondence are kept while needed to review and respond, manage a relationship or meet applicable legal and record-keeping needs. There is currently no fixed automatic expiry for enquiries. You can ask us about or request deletion of information you have sent us, subject to applicable law and any information we need to retain.

Future HQventures products

HQventures may introduce products with different features, such as accounts, paid services, file uploads or AI-assisted processing. These are examples of possible future features, not a statement that they are all available today.

Before a product uses personal information in a materially different way, we will explain the information involved, purposes, providers, retention, choices and relevant terms in an updated or product-specific notice. Where required, we will ask for a separate choice or consent before that processing begins. This policy does not itself give permission for unspecified future uses.

Advertising

Advertising placements are not currently enabled on YorpHives. An AdSense ownership-verification tag or ads.txt file may be present; those do not by themselves display ads. If advertising is introduced, we will update the notice and provide the choices or consent controls required for the relevant visitors before enabling it.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information; object to or restrict certain processing; receive information in a portable format; withdraw consent; or complain to a relevant data-protection authority. Some rights have exceptions.

To make a request, contact us below. We may need reasonable information to verify your identity and find the relevant records. Because analytics are aggregated or use a random tab identifier, we may not be able to identify a particular visitor's records. Turning analytics off stops future collection by that client; it does not automatically identify and erase earlier aggregate counts.

We use information to respond to requests and enquiries, run and secure the service, and meet applicable obligations. Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, this may include consent, steps requested before a contract, compliance with legal obligations, or legitimate interests such as keeping the service secure, as appropriate to the activity. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it.

Children

If a child has sent us personal information and you are their parent or guardian, please contact us. Depending on local law, a parent or guardian may need to make choices or requests for a child.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. For material changes, we will provide an appropriate notice and obtain a fresh choice or consent where required before the new processing begins.

Contact us

For a privacy question, complaint or request concerning YorpHives or HQventures, email connect@yorphives.com. Please include enough detail for us to understand your request, but do not send passwords or sensitive documents unless we specifically need them and arrange a secure method.
