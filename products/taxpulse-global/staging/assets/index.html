    In Plain English

    What Your Paycheck Breakdown Really Means

    Out of every ₹100 you earn, you keep ₹89.60 in your pocket, ₹8.40 goes to Income Tax, and ₹2.00 goes into your mandatory retirement savings (EPF).

    Gross Pay vs. Net Take-Home Pay

    Gross Pay is your total salary on paper before any deductions. Net Take-Home Pay is the actual cash that lands in your bank account.

    Standard Deduction

    A flat tax-free discount the government gives every salaried worker automatically (like ₹75,000 in India or $16,100 in the US) before calculating tax.

    Effective vs. Marginal Tax Rate

    Effective Rate is the overall % of your total salary paid in tax. Marginal Rate is the tax % applied only to the next extra dollar/rupee you earn.

    Short-Term (STCG) vs. Long-Term (LTCG)

    Profits from selling stocks or property held for a longer period (usually over 12 or 24 months) qualify for lower Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) tax rates.

    DTAA (Double Taxation Avoidance)

    An agreement between two countries so you don't pay tax twice on the same foreign income—tax paid abroad is credited against your home tax bill.

    Presumptive Tax (Sec 44ADA) & Gratuity

    Sec 44ADA lets Indian freelancers pay tax on only half (50%) of their income. Gratuity is a lump-sum loyalty bonus paid when leaving a job after 5+ years.
