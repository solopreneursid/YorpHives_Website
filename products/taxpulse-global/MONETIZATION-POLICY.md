# TaxPulse monetization controls
Preview: no advertising, tracking, affiliate redirects, registration or payments. Never load ad scripts or affiliate tracking into calculation flows. No financial input or result is shared with a partner.
Production prerequisites: independent statutory scope approval, owner approval, relevant publisher review, documented consent/privacy behavior, regional disclosures and vendor review. Monetize eligible public informational pages first; reserve calculation and result screens for uninterrupted use. Disclose affiliate relationships adjacent to links, mark paid links sponsored, no recommendations driven by commission. No automatic ad activation on build or release. No payment processing or premium access until an explicit product decision. Track aggregate traffic only after the agreed consent policy; never collect raw salary amounts.
