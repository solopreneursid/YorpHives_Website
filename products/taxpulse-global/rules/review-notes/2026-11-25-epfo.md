# India payroll review: EPFO ceiling change
Observed 2026-11-25. Independent verification pending.
Official PIB announcement: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2313790&lang=2&reg=48
The Ministry reported that the statutory wage ceiling for mandatory coverage increased from INR 15,000 to INR 25,000 effective 17 September 2026. The legacy salary and contribution models only accept an explicitly confirmed EPF wage up to INR 15,000; they do not model the new ceiling or mid-year transition. Do not infer an employee's contribution base from gross salary or present the old scope as current universal law. Verify Gazette notification, applicability, transition, wage definition, employee and employer contribution treatment and fixtures before changing the model. New CTC tool takes separately confirmed annual payroll amounts. No approval or production activation implied.
