# Future-period evidence, 2026-11-25
## United States OASDI
SSA's published 2027 taxable maximum is USD 192,300; the October 26, 2026 Federal Register notice confirms applicability to remuneration paid in 2027. SSA's fact sheet compares USD 184,500 in 2026 and USD 192,300 in 2027. The 6.2% employee rate remains a scoped parameter. Sources: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-10-26/html/2026-21796.htm ; https://www.ssa.gov/cola/factsheets/2027.html ; https://www.ssa.gov/oact/COLA/cbb.html . The 2027 record must not replace the 2026 record or imply support for a complete 2027 salary calculation.
## Australia
Income Tax Rates Act 1986, compilation dated 2026-07-01, and the ATO explanation confirm the first taxable band rate of 15% for 2026–27 and 14% from 2027–28. Sources: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A03348/2026-07-01/2026-07-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html ; https://www.ato.gov.au/about-ato/new-legislation/in-detail/individuals/personal-income-tax-new-tax-cuts-for-every-australian-taxpayer . Existing historical data already distinguishes these years.
