How to trim

  1. Select a video your browser can play.
  2. Choose a start and end time, up to 30 seconds apart.
  3. Keep this tab visible while the clip records locally, then download it.

Good to know

Processing uses native browser recording, so it takes roughly as long as the selected clip. Output is WebM or MP4 depending on your browser, and quality or exact cut points may differ. Sound is preserved where supported. Some mobile and Safari browsers may not support this feature.
