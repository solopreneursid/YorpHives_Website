    How to use this tool

    1. Enter your information and choose any options.
    2. Run the tool to see the result.
    3. Download the result when it is ready.

    Good to know

    Enter page numbers such as 1,3-5. Pages follow the order you enter. PDF page operations may not preserve interactive forms, bookmarks, or signatures. Encrypted PDFs are not supported. Each file can be up to 20 MB; selected files can total up to 40 MB and 500 pages. Processing happens on your device.
