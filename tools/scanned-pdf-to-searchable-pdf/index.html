How to use

  1. Choose a PDF made from scans or photos.
  2. Select Make PDF Searchable.
  3. Download and search your new PDF.

What to expect

Uses local English OCR, which can make mistakes in handwriting, small text or poor-quality scans. The original PDF is unchanged. The output is reconstructed page by page, so fonts, forms, signatures and links may not be preserved. Maximum 5 pages per batch.
