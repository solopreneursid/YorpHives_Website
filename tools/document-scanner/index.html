How it works

  1. Select a clear JPG, PNG or WebP image of paper.
  2. Move four corner handles around the actual paper edges.
  3. Choose a clean-up mode and download PDF or JPG.

Privacy and limitations

All perspective correction runs in your browser. PDFs contain scanned page images rather than editable text. For sensitive documents, verify the crop includes only the information you intend to share.
