How to use this tool

  1. Upload a PDF file.
  2. Select Low, Best, or High compression.
  3. Download your PDF when it is ready.

Good to know

Best for scanned or image-heavy PDFs. Compression may turn pages into images, so searchable text, links, forms, annotations and signatures can be lost. Files that do not become smaller are returned unchanged. Limit: 15 MB and 35 pages. Processing stays in your browser.
