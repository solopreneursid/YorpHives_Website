How to use this tool

Supports Latin, Devanagari, Bengali, Gujarati, Gurmukhi, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Tamil, Telugu, Arabic, Hebrew, Thai, Sinhala and Myanmar scripts. Unsupported characters are reported before creating a PDF. On first use, about 2.6 MB of fonts is downloaded from this site; your text and files stay on your device.

  1. Enter your information and choose any options.
  2. Run the tool to see the result.
  3. Download the result when it is ready.

Good to know

A visible, handwritten-style typed mark only—not a cryptographic or identity-verified digital signature. Check whether your recipient accepts it. Changing a signed PDF may invalidate its signatures. Processing happens locally in your browser.
