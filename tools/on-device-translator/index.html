How this works

  1. Choose the source and target language.
  2. Paste your text and select “Translate on this device”.
  3. If necessary, allow your browser to download its language pack, then copy the result.

What if translation is unavailable?

This browser or language pair may not support the API. Your text stays editable; you can copy it and use your browser's built-in page translation tools where available. YorpHives does not silently send it to a cloud translation service.

Translation quality and supported languages depend on the browser. A locally cached model may work offline, but an initial download may require internet access.
