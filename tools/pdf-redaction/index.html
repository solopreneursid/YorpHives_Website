    How to redact

    1. Choose a PDF and wait for its preview.
    2. Drag a box across every private detail to remove.
    3. Redact and download the new flattened PDF.

    Privacy and limitations

    Nothing is uploaded. The new PDF is rasterized, so source text and interactive content will not remain available. The tool supports up to 12 pages. Password-protected PDFs may not work. For sensitive legal disclosures, review the redaction carefully.
